# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two primary audiences:

1. **Autoimmune patients** (priority surface: `/ai`): Adults with Hashimoto, Crohn's, psoriasis, and related inflammatory conditions. Often exhausted, frustrated with quick consultations, and seeking someone who will listen to their full story. They need confidence that the practitioner understands the biological terrain, not just symptoms.

2. **Families of neurodivergent children** (main site): Parents and caregivers of children with TEA (ASD), TDAH (ADHD), and T21 (Down syndrome). They want evidence-based, integrative nutrition that respects their child's individuality. Emotional context: hope mixed with vigilance.

Context for both: visitors arrive via referrals, social media, or search. They are evaluating trust and expertise before committing to a consultation or treatment program.

## Product Purpose

Marketing surfaces for Dra. Lauriana de Magalhães Silva's nutrition practice. The `/ai` autoimmune landing page converts visitors into 120-day treatment patients. The main site (`/`, `/en/`, `/es/`, `/pt/`) builds long-term credibility for neurodevelopmental nutrition services.

Success looks like: a visitor feels understood, sees the practitioner's expertise as grounded in science yet deeply human, and takes the next step (WhatsApp contact, email, or booking).

## Brand Personality

Grounded, Human, Precise.

- **Grounded**: No miracle promises. The voice is steady, earthy, and rooted in biochemistry.
- **Human**: Warmth without sentimentality. The practitioner is present, accessible, and listens.
- **Precise**: Credentials, data, and process are communicated clearly. Structure and clarity build trust.

Emotional goals: confidence (this person knows the science), relief (someone finally sees the whole picture), and trust (the process is transparent).

## Anti-references

- Corporate medical white-and-blue templates (cold, institutional, generic)
- Wellness influencer beige-and-sage monoculture (undifferentiated, generic "clean living")
- Generic SaaS landing pages with hero metrics, identical card grids, and eyebrow labels on every section
- All-caps tracked section kickers and numbered section markers as default scaffolding
- Gradient text and glassmorphism as decorative defaults
- Side-stripe borders as card accents

## Design Principles

1. **Precision without coldness** — Data and credentials presented with human warmth. Science is rigorous; the delivery is gentle.
2. **The body as terrain, not machine** — Organic, living visual language. No mechanical metaphors or clinical sterility.
3. **Trust through transparency** — Clear pricing, clear process, no hidden steps. What you see is what you get.
4. **Accessibility as care** — Cognitive accessibility, reduced motion, and high contrast are not afterthoughts; they are part of the service.
5. **Grounded confidence** — Neither timid nor aggressive. The design holds its own without shouting.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum for all text and interactive elements
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected for all animations
- High contrast options for cognitive accessibility
- Clear focus indicators and readable type sizes
- Alt text that carries voice, not just description
